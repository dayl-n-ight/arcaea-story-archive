The day after, and after another combat...
Back at Base, she thinks to herself in her quarters while reading.

The Light she's seen...it isn't something Coda can identify for her, and it isn't in any of the military's 
records either. What's more, though Coda has seen it—nobody else has ever been able to attest to it. 
Rather, it can't be kept as a permanent image or video in Coda's drives...

It's an anomaly of Space, it seems. Sometimes, she's been called crazy for talking of it, for looking for it—

But that her C.O.D.A. corroborates its existence—her comrades have to believe her:

They have to believe that there is some blue light in the far reaches of Space, which Nonoka is certain 
is full of beauty.

"Good job during the deployment today," Mitsuko says as she enters Nonoka's quarters. The friends 
meet one another's eyes.

"Something up?" Nonoka asks. Momentarily, their mutual gaze is interrupted by Coda lazily floating past. 
Mitsuko's Coda watches it on a tilted axis.

----
"We're heading toward the area of Space your Light is at," says Mitsuko lightly, leaning against a set of 
cabinets and looking out her friend's window. Her friend shivers.

"No way...Why!? Is...Info is investigating it!?" Nonoka asks, almost pleading. "C-Can I transfer to Info Class!?"

"We're not investigating it, we're not researchers Nonoka," Mitsuko replies. Her voice is half-apologetic. 
"We're soldiers."

"Damn it! I've made all the wrong life choices!" Nonoka complains, unserious.

"At least you had choices to make, RS-R Nonoka," mutters Coda.

"What's with you? You don't understand jokes?"

----
"Anyway," Mitsuko interrupts the spat, "we'll still be a lot closer to it...You want me to help you out?"

"...Help me out how?" answers Nonoka. She pauses, and glances at the two robots floating through her 
quarters. "...Should we turn off our Codas?"

"Nah. They can only report misconduct. They're programmed that way."

"...And...misconduct...is NOT what you're proposing, Mitsuko?"

"Do you want my help or not?"

And, knowing how well Mitsuko knows her...

"Yes!"

She answers hurriedly, waiting not a moment longer.

----
"You can be part of Scouting when we get there," says Mitsuko, coming to sit beside Nonoka on her bed. 
"Command will be relieved to hear it, really. Trust me, they'd LOVE if they could get RS-Rs pulling double 
duty combat and scouting..."

"'If'? So I can't?" asks Nonoka.

"You're more like an RSSS-R, so...they're a lot more willing with you."

"Heh."

"Honestly...why are you so interested in that 'light' anyway?"

"The way you say that...you're saying it as if it's a 'word' aren't you. Call it 'Light', capital L." Upon hearing 
this from its charge, Coda oscillates in exasperation.

"Pft, put respect on its name?" asks Mitsuko. "Really though, why do you want to get to it so bad?"

----
"Because only Coda and I can see it..." answers Nonoka lightly, falling backward onto her bed. "Don't you 
think that's nuts? Maybe it's calling out to me. Uuughh, Mitsuko! You don't know how much I think 
about the Light!"

"Well that's scary. And yeah, I don't."

"No way, it's not scary at all."

"Okay so what if it's calling you? What then?"

"Then I want to talk to it."

"Like you 'talk' to opposing Runners?"

"Not like that."

"A heart-to-heart, huh..." saying this, Mitsuko looks down to where her friend's heart is. "...Well in that case, 
I hope the Light is, unlike you, a saint."

----
"Unlike me??"

"Unlike you."

"Really? Really really?" Nonoka picks herself up onto her elbows, smirking teasingly. "Really unlike me? 
Do you even know how many times I've saved your butt?"

"No."

"It's twelve times. Twelve, Mitsuko! A dozen!"

"Wow, that's a lot of times. Consider this me saving you one time, then."

Nonoka watches her for a moment...
...then reaches up and embraces her whilst squealing.

She laughs, Mitsuko laughs back and lightly pushes her away with no strength behind her hands..

A moment of friends continues, there, in the dark of Space.
