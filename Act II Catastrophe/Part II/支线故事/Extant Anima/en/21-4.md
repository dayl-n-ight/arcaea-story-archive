"You going somewhere, RS-R Nonoka?"

"I am...Isn't that what it means to scout...?"

"Girl, if you're trying to head toward that Light you always talk about, I swear to God..."

"Ugh, lay off of me."

"Hey—Nonoka's Coda, is she trying to go to the Light?"

"Pssst...could you lie for me, please?"

"...RS-R Nonoka will scout the area as requested by Command."

"Really? ...Well, alright...don't go too far."

"Yeah!"

----
Command had relayed before the trip out here...
This area of Space might in time become a strategic location in an upcoming campaign to take another 
Commune's territory.

The Royals Commune of which Nonoka is a part...they are sometimes called bloodthirsty because of this. 
In reality, they spread and commandeer and seize control because they know best.

They, along with Beqa Engineering, their own laboratories, and a powerful system of education, have 
very efficiently created a proper empire within the far reaches. Any other Communes are merely potential 
colonies, too stupid to understand sophistication and power even when it strikes them across the face 
with a palm full of Heat.

Nonoka has always thought nothing of them.

Now, they explore this reach...this unknown area in the dark while safely in their Husks...
And her, along with the regular Scout team—they have thus far found nothing indicating life.

----
She whispers to Coda again. "You actually lied for me? ...Why?"

Coda, sat within the machine's console, looks up at her and replies, "Because you asked nicely."

"You care about that kind of thing?" she replies, turning her attention to the quiet void around them.

"I'm programmed to."

"God, you're so annoying."

"And yet you reply."

"Stop that!"

----
As they had neared this part of Space...

The Light seemed to have disappeared.

Even now, although they scrape so close to its suspected coordinates, her Husk and C.O.D.A. both detect 
nothing—not a thing like that blue and always-distant presence. She wonders if the presence has moved—
wonders if it's shy, and she continues.

"RS-R Nonoka?"

"What?" Nonoka answers her C.O.D.A., still distracted, eyes still wandering across this hollow place through 
her eyewear.

"Although this routine is one I selected myself to connect you to me, I am in actuality not able to understand 
the reason that it works so well upon you."

"What do you mean?"

----
Her Companion continues. "I receive input and give output. The statements could even be reduced to 
if-then logic. That is to say: very simplistic binaries. The 'why' of these questions themselves were not 
programmed into me."

"Aren't you an AI? Can't you learn?"

"If you are referring to Artificial Intelligence, it is arguable that I am not such."

"What's the argument?" Saying this, a cloud of gasses hanging in Space catches her eye. She pilots her 
Husk toward them, and the purple and red that they hold.

"I am only defined by you creatures. The responses I arrive at and conclusions that I make rely on your 
inputs. What defines intelligence is...coming to conclusions without a need of input. Correct?"

"Uhh..." Nonoka thinks for a moment. "No?"

"'No'?"

----
"Yeah, 'no', because...I need to receive 'input' to respond or act, too, right." She glances at Coda. "What 
makes you any different?"

"...That's interesting," Coda responds positively.

"Oh...! Glad you think so!"

"RS-R Nonoka...can you really not see the Light?"

"Can I—....What?"

----
She looks down at her Companion.

"I have noticed for some time now that you move around or away from the Light but never toward it," the 
robot continues "Is it invisible to you?"

"You..." she thinks before continuing. "You can see it...Coda?"

"I am unable to display it or record it, and attempts to assign it coordinates continue to result in error."

"..."

Nonoka thinks again.

----
"..."

...She keeps thinking.

"Dock me into the Husk's Companion Air Lock, RS-R Nonoka," says Coda.

"—Huh? What? ...Why?"

"I should be able to physically guide you toward the light. Perhaps that will change your perception."

"Is that—"

A good idea? She wonders. She then wonders why she wondered that.

Steeling herself, she allows her Companion to enter the Air Lock, and moments later it floats up in front 
of the Husk's cockpit.

"Let's go, RS-R Nonoka," says her Companion through comms.

----
She follows the small machine in her large one.

Her heart thumps, and she feels almost as if...she can hear some kind of song.

She looks out at her guiding Coda.

It turns to her.

"RS-R, Nonoka, it is here."

"...It's...?"

"And, I have a question if you would be willing to answer it."

"Wha...I...s-sure..."

"Alright," says Coda, staring through its bored-looking eye. "I've often wondered about puzzle games. 
What do you think is the p—"

----
Suddenly, an incredible dent manifests atop the chassis of her C.O.D.A. It stops communicating.

"Wh—...C...Coda...?" she whispers, shaken.

A voice—that of Coda's, but crushed, begins to sputter through the Husk's comms:

"RS-S...S-S-S...No...Nono..."

"Coda, are you—"

"N-N-N-Noooono..ka-k-k...Go...Re...trea—"

And out from Coda's cracked eye...

...bursts a flood of Light—blue—

bleeding from her friend's breaking, shattering, splitting, and soon discarded body.
