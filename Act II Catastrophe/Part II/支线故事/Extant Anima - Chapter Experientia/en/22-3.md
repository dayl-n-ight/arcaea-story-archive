Mitsuko swore under her breath as she watched swathes and swathes of Husks burst throughout space 
as Nonoka's vessel tore at terrible speed through them.

"Stop..." she uttered. "Stop, Nonoka!! You should come after ME!"

And again, Nonoka's voice bled into Mitsuko's Husk. Hearing laughter in her tone, her friend answered:

"A mirror to your voice.

M—...M-Mmmiitsuko, you h-h-have to come to me...!"

Mitsuko grit her teeth, and gripped the controls of her Husk tightly.

----
Another engagement wouldn't succeed. According to reports, Nonoka had been tirelessly fighting for 
days. Many pilots better than Mitsuko had already fallen. She had fought Nonoka already and had done 
nothing but lose one of her own Husk's limbs... this practice was futile, and Mitsuko was dreaming.

She still wanted to believe in spite of it all...
She wanted to believe that her voice, if any, could reach the other girl.

But she could not deny, as the destruction spread, and as that Color bled more and more through 
space—people screaming and rapidly sending out distress signals...

...she could not deny the beauty of what she was seeing. And at that, she saw Nonoka's face in her 
screen again.

----
"Y-You see. It."

Saying that, color curled out from beneath Nonoka's hair where her darkened right eye now sat, and 
Mitsuko felt herself going pale. Nonoka grinned again.

"So. Come," she said, and something new burst out with overwhelming force from her distant Husk.

----
A hundred more pilots fell: a rainbow of bursting, crackling, and the dead, and it and all the destruction 
and color Nonoka had brought over the last days swirled down to one point on the horizon.

It felt like a heart beating through space. It felt as if something wanted to be born.

It drew Mitsuko's eyes...

----
At the center of the vortex Nonoka had created...

...just behind her Husk a twisted and blue throne, awesome and immense, was crafted in the hollow of space.
