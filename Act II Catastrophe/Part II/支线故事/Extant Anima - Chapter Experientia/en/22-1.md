Something strange burst out from the depths of space. Vision itself cracked in two, and a new light and 
color bled out from there: a new terror.

Nonoka was born anew. Nonoka felt her life soaring out her body.

She became one with her Husk. She set her sights on the unit she had come here with.

She showed them the Light, and left them dead.

----
There is no lesson to be had in this story.

In existence, infinite and unending, there are infinite and unending possibilities.

The ace of a powerful empire became swallowed by an alien light—

Horror was born of her, and spread throughout the galaxy.

----
Reports of her corruption came soon. People fled and Mitsuko—who Nonoka began to persistently call 
for—retreated too, and hid until she was inevitably dragged out as the chaos grew worse.

The light began to consume this world, and as its herald, Nonoka needed to encourage it—to encourage 
that all and forever beauty she had seen and so she reached. She reached, and reached for more and 
colored the sky more, and she knew at the end of every death, the sorrows could create for the Color—
for the Light a throne. And it will live. It will live. It will live, you have seen it there, at the center and calling: 
it will be, and it will be praised.

From this:

A new star could dawn.

And the world could be perfect.

----
There is no lesson to be had in this story.

In existence, infinite and unending, there are infinite and unending possibilities.

And with certainty, this possibility exists too:

After the herald would perish...

...she could awaken in another world.
