"We aren't operating on sense anymore, RB-R Mitsuko!" her superior had shouted this as he shook her 
by her shoulders. "The only name she called for was yours. Get in your Husk and fly to her NOW!"

With that said...

She now sat in her Husk's cockpit, waiting to deploy. Her Coda looked up at her from its place in the 
console—to her it looked sympathetic.

The destruction thus far had just been undeniable...and that strange color...

...It had already reached their sector after the retreat, and all it had come in contact with had become 
twisted. Things were born and undone in the color—in that Light, and RS-R Nonoka was its herald.

----
"RB-R Mitsuko, you're coming with?" said a friend of hers as she waited in the station's airlock with sweat 
beading down her face. "...Hm. Maybe she won't try to get you."

"That's what...Command is hoping..." With this reply, Mitsuko tried to steady her trembling hands on the 
console.

The airlock opened.

Outside, a canvas of blue and red spread throughout space. Mitsuko and the others—five other pilots all 
headed out.

----
In time, the station they had left was far behind...reinforcements of another Commune would meet them 
where the active battlefield had been determined.

The target was alone as ever...Nonoka had, by herself, torn through faction after faction, army after army, 
and unit after unit. Her path had been clear: toward the station where Mitsuko had fled with the others. 
Even had that not been obvious...she had made strange requests for Mitsuko time and again.

Now Mitsuko looked out into space.
She closed her eyes.
When she opened them again, her comrades' Husks were all broken and spread around her in space, 
dashed with that corrupting color, and a voice—a voice of that same color came horrid through her Coda.

"Mii...tsuko... You're here!"

"No...noka..." Mitsuko replied.

She'd heard that Nonoka's Coda seemed to be missing...and yet she could still communicate.
Somehow, that fact shook her more readily than her fallen fellow soldiers around her.

----
![CG: 22-2-2](../images/22-2-2_cg.jpg)

----
"It needs to hatch...!" Nonoka exclaimed, her face appearing in her friend's cockpit display. Mitsuko had 
already seen it in reports...but seeing Nonoka's new appearance moving, speaking, and so changed...

She wanted to reach out to her, really. She wanted to be embraced, and as she began to feel that—she 
shook her head violently, and reason won out. There had been reports of exactly this: of a compelling 
force when encountering this "herald" or the color...

Mitsuko steadied herself...and readied her weapons.

"Good, you'll help the light—" Nonoka began, and Mitsuko interrupted her.

"I'll snuff it out. That's all, Nonoka."

Nonoka only grinned, and the two at once began a whirling duel.

----
Mitsuko did the best she could—
She lost her Husk's arm in the process.

Nonoka's presence, more than her piloting, was simply too overwhelming... Mitsuko repeatedly would 
lag in her own movements—gaze too long or begin to laugh in spite of herself at nothing. Nothing.

Truthfully, it was no battle. This twisted Nonoka was playing a game, and whatever grand vision she had 
for the new sky of the world...it could not be understood, it only had to be stopped.

----
So she cried out to her friend. She slammed Husk against Husk and in their robotic armor screamed at 
Nonoka, "You've got to stop! Don't you remember anything!?"

And to that she received the prompt reply: "I remember everything, and it's why I d...do anything, Mitsuko."

Before Mitsuko could argue this, reinforcements arrived...

...and Nonoka split from her, in order to meet them.
