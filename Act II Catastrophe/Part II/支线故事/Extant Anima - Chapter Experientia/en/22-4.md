Columns rose up from the void—columns of a gorgeous and compelling blue, but crouched in her 
humanoid machine Mitsuko narrowed her eyes from them.

Her friend stood before the newly formed and cosmic-towering throne, its back and arms stretching to 
wretched distances. Or, Nonoka's Husk stood there, and Nonoka sat within it. Even from here, Mitsuko 
could feel her friend's smile.

She told herself to rise. She gripped the controls at the console and piloted well. She equipped her lance.

As cries of fear and happiness bled through the comms of the robotic partner sitting in her Husk's console, 
Mitsuko pushed forward. Her partner Coda almost seemed afraid, and still Mitsuko pushed forward.

----
The situation had not changed in her favor. Rank S-Runners like Nonoka were a gulf above Mitsuko's 
Rank, and now Nonoka had changed horribly. They would fight again, and Mitsuko would fail.

Entire forces were gone now—whole Communes had come across the last days and their Husks and 
warships now made tombstones of the grave battlefield. And, still, the Color expanded. The appearance 
of the throne had only seemed to encourage it.

But still she pushed forward, and soon she met her friend before that throne—to duel again.

----
A horrid clash—

As their weapons met in power, her Husk began to rattle throughout itself. The two turned about one 
another with Nonoka's swipes and shots in (to Mitsuko's eyes) obvious signs of "play" rather than "pressure". 
Nonoka allowed every parry, and her laughter and color would now and then sharply erupt from Mitsuko's 
Coda. Sweat formed at Mitsuko's brow, and her heart began to twist.

Even now—

—she wished, somehow, that talking could pierce the Husk before her.

But only a weapon could do such, and in time Nonoka's play proved fatal—and Mitsuko's lance found 
its mark.

----
The weapon tore violent through Nonoka's hull and body, and she and her craft shuddered.

In the twisting cosmos, two girls drifted surrounded by strangest light, and sky-painted horizons.

As Nonoka succumbed...

...Mitsuko realized that her body, too, had taken that light. Wheezing, she looked bleary-eyed through her 
screens: to the wicked smile that Nonoka still wore. Her Coda went silent. No voices were calling anymore.

----
And Nonoka spoke to her a final time—as the pieces of Mitsuko's Husk cracked and splintered and 
exposed her cockpit to the airless void of space:

"Thank you," Nonoka told her, "for breaking this shell."

Mitsuko's eyes widened. Her breath was stolen, and she took in the final sight with the last seconds 
of her life:

That of the Color blue crawling out from Nonoka's husk... and from there swiftly churning throughout 
the galaxy.

The throne sat empty, and the impaled and dying herald smiled before it, a new and pure dark star 
forming above and beginning to scream throughout the skies in ecstacy.
