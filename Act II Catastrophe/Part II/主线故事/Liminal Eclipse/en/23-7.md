# 23-7

![CG: 23-7](../images/23-7_cg.jpg)

An expanse between places,
entered at the utterance of a wish.
A call beyond the stars,
heard with heart and mind.
And in a moment the loss of a soul.
A soul sent tumbling through new ether.

Wanting to hear the voice of her friend again, Vita's soul is sent away from her body.

----
A fierce blaze of white drives around her. She tries to hold steady, but it feels like weight and gravity
are gripping at her form.

The tumult of borders of two places clash upon her and her mind quakes.

----
But, in time...

...the chaos quiets down. Her ephemeral body finds itself in a new "dark". A space-like dark, familiar.
She can see other places here, and new and white stars:
she can see the shifting existence of faraway expanses.
She can see the light of a seemingly perfect and distant world.
And before it—

A girl with one hand left—
A girl with two colored hair and eyes, akin to hers—

Maya drifts, almost shattered, in the dark there.

----
Vita shouts, firmly, "Maya!" and there, and suddenly, a familiar and unknowable color surrounds her 
and to see it, to feel it, Vita shivers and goes cold.

She lifts her translucent hand.

The color wraps her fingers. It winds her arm and neck and tugs. She tugs herself forward, gnashing her teeth.

And, Maya reaches back.

----
Light and shadow fly between the girls, and dashes all color out and, steady, their hands meet: like it's real.

Vita can feel her heart beating, and Maya's.

Between the stars, they pull together and hold one another in the silence.

----
It's true.

Another story is about to end, and Vita knows it well.

"Funny, that," Maya whispers with a dry voice. "Once you got here, I remembered everything I'd ever forgot..."

"Maya," Vita answers, "did...did that woman do this to you?" She asks this,
looking down at the other girl's fractured body.

Maya shakes her head. "It's circumstances," she remarks, "that's it...
And I'm sorry to add another sad story to yours."

"..."

"What are you going to do now?" Maya asks.

----
"You still don't worry enough about yourself," Vita replies.

"Sure... Hey, answer me?"

"About what I'll do...? I...don't know. I don't..." she pauses, and slowly brings her eyes to her friend's.
"I don't know why I even tried to find you."

Maya bids Vita to briefly let her go. Shaking, Maya brings her hand to her side...and presents a tome. 
"For this?" asks Maya with sad humor.

----
"I wasn't," Vita says flatly.

"You should have," Maya replies, smiling.

"Maybe it was to save you..." Vita answers, and Maya loses her smile. "I didn't want to lose you, too."

"..."

----
"Then you won't. Shall I let you save me?"

"What...do you mean?"

"We've talked about this, Vita—" Maya answers, and Vita winces. "What we are to each other. You remember?"

"'Remember', that...isn't funny."

"No, it isn't at all..."

Vita is quiet. Maya is quiet a while...

"If what memory I have can be carried on through you," says Maya, "with you, I wouldn't fade away."

Whispering, Vita asks, "...Are you going to fall apart without that?"

----
Slowly, Maya nods.

"Am I...supposed to accept that?"

"...Vita..."

"...No, I understand." Vita says this, and she affirms it too in herself. She repeats: "I understand."


----
Vita...grabs a hold of the tome as well, and they each hold Maya's journal between them.

Steady, Vita nods once.

"...Maya," she answers. "I'll bring you with me."

And as she says this...

...memory begins to twist around them.
