// Maya, 42. //

// I can certainly confirm it by now: the flow of time from here and in relation to Arcaea seems to be strange.

In simplest terms I suspect that my voice is somehow calling "back" to my friend,
and that "time" progression on her side is set at an inconsistent scale to mine.
Sometimes an hour here is a day there, but in other times a minute instead is an hour.
To her, the world breaking apart and lights cascading was recent.
For me, it was quite some time ago.

However: as our voices meet, that conversation will not be stretched through time.

It reminds me of time dilation. //

----
// More importantly—I was right: weather has begun in Arcaea. So, now that she and I have "met"
roughly from when I was taken, her voice may now call to me from "the future"?
How will Arcaea change?

As for that strange woman: she had spoken a little of her ambition before taking me here.
She mentioned how Arcaea was broken, and that her hands could right it.
Does "weather" make that world more "natural"?

—I can't easily believe her. //

----
// The woman spoke like she knew me—from at a distance. She may have obsessions, delusions.

Is my writing too elaborate? When I was younger—alive? When I was back home, I would sometimes wonder,
"Am I very pretentious?" Maybe my writing is so dry it's at risk of starting a fire. //

A thought came out from another side as Maya wandered the halls:

"Maya! Guess what?"

"She let you lead one of your trips?"

"She let me lead—oh wow! Yes!" Receiving that thought, Maya put away her pen and smiled.

----
Correspondence between them was regular now. Maya shared what she learned about where
she had been taken, and her other half kept her up to date on the goings on in Arcaea.

On another day:

"Hey Maya, when you say phantoms, do you mean they're ghosts...?"

"I mean more like...like images you aren't sure you're seeing: they're like mirages."

"Oh! Mirages!"

"Mhm."

But surely feelings are only those. Feelings don't make anything "real" in a real world.
