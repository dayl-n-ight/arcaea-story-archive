// Maya, 108. //

// It may surprise you: I still remember my name. I write it every day, after all—even with one arm remaining.
That only makes writing difficult.

I don't have long.
I've researched what I myself have written down repeatedly, testing against what I can see around me.
But, my failing form remains a mystery.

"Phantoms" are unlike me: not falling apart; this may be a condition of what I "am"—
not an act of that woman. That woman finds me precious; she'll likely be upset to find me as dust when she returns.

Glass dust. Hm.

—I have something to say to you, Vita.

You, I can remember very clearly. //

----
...Maya's story is to end, and soon.
Falling apart and forgetting... In truth, with such impossibly tragic circumstances:
God could not have predicted this.

But, the girl is right...
What she is is glass and lingering memory, taken outside of Arcaea.

A fragment. Nothing more.

----
...In another place:

Other fragments drift within some gloom.
Within the dark, and underneath the world.
A girl named Vita holds something between her hands.
And, she carefully puts it away. She looks up and into a darkened landscape.

...She hasn't heard the voice of her friend in many days.

----
Vita had told her that she needed to take care of herself and only received light reassurance in reply.
But...Maya was fading.

So, if she can't hear that voice ever again...
If she can't hear another voice ever again...
Then that is reality: despairing.

----
—She thinks: no.
It can't be. And it won't. Believing that, in another place—that alone is enough.

Though this is absurd: Arcaea will hear it.

An unsaid wish is enough...
Two hearts, one, will meet by heart—with the help of an innate connection of mind.

This is old power, and also the sympathy of a quiet god. Maya, Vita: another hears you both...

Now, let another tell her tale.
