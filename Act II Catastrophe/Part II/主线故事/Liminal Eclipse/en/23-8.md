"That's stupid—it's stupid military talk."

"...Ha, you know? It is."

As Vita speaks...
"It was always stupid."

The words between them begin to blur.

A distant star twinkles, and another, and another.

A flower of crystal blossoms from Maya's body, and light eases from the splits in her form.

The two girls each close their eyes.

And Maya's memories, the memories of Vita, begin to pass on.

----
The confused world, the strange terrors and whims of godly things...

They all stop between them.

Here is the story that has been, from first to last:

----
Born into a spacefaring world. Born with a gift.

Raised to raise the flag of a nation. Raised to command countless pathways.

Raised into ignorance, Vita came to die when she opened those pathways to a voice she should never have trusted.

And death, and death, and at a new border of white a broken world's flaws once more came to fore.

Misshapen made, by a misshapen heart, Arcaea took Vita's memories—but could not disperse them.

Those memories began to feel and from there began to think, and they radiated with pain.

So, Arcaea granted them shelter in the form of a body of glass.

----
That other body awakened in another place, apart from Vita.
It woke with the same feelings that had enticed its creation, and it suffered.

Maya's memories...
and Vita's are the same.

A color begins to shift around them again, rushed away by light.

More flowers blossom from Maya's form—

And the worlds all around them begin to spiral in a rush.

----
Maya and Vita open their eyes to a torrent of light. Vita reaches forward with her other hand to grip
Maya's shoulder, to embrace her again as the place in between begins to collapse and rend.

Light and sound push past them overwhelming as Vita's soul is dragged back to the world of glass.
She holds her other half tightly with eyes narrowed—

—her gaze fixes on the smile on Maya's face, as her other half's body splinters and breaks,
and the glittering of glass trails their journey.

Stars continue to scream past them. The noise of space ripping asunder suddenly erases to silence.
Vita holds, and holds, until there is nothing to hold on to left.

In another world, halls have gone quiet again.

Vita opens her eyes—head and heart full of old and new memory.
Down, in Arcaea.

----
The dark again... Here, Vita centers herself to the distorted world.

She lifts a book in her hand.

"..."

----
She holds the book out before her, open at the center before a new stretch of empty pages.
She stares down at it, and reads the last words:


// Thank you for calling back to me. //


Vita holds the book steady, and her lips tight. Her face...she tries to keep it still, and she breathes out hotly.

Slowly...

She says nothing.

----
"Vita...what have you got there?"

Vita turns a little. She looks at the book again.

Paging through it, she considers her answer.

She remembers the pages, but still turns through them—passing her gaze over the words.

...She finishes her thoughts. She closes the book.

She turns, she meets her companion's eyes.

And, she gives her answer.
