"You can actually hear me!?" The "voice" that Maya sent out through the skies was a loud one.
The one on the receiving end must have flinched to hear it.

After assuredly flinching, the other replied: "This isn't just..!? I'm...I'm not just...imagining this!? Oh...!"

"Yes...Yes...!" Maya repeated. "Yes..." aloud, she said it. With her heart, with her throat.
"Thank y... Yes...yes..." ...She began to cry again.

"You're...You're crying...?"

"..."

...Maya sobbed for her answer.

----
Silence stretched between them for some time...

The other voice eventually asked her, "You're...definitely real, right?"

"I am," answered Maya. "What's your... What is your name?"

"Ah—name...?"

"Your...rank and desig—Mm, your insignia? Sorry," Maya asked properly.

"'Insignia'?" the other girl repeated, and her confusion was shared across their thoughts.
In Maya's chest, a cold hand began to slowly close its fingers around her heart...

The other girl did not remember.
The bond of the mind was there—but this girl did not "remember"...Realizing that, acknowledging that...
...a small crack appeared on Maya's cheek.

...Foolishly, Maya tried again.

----
"Th—The...Imperium, do you know it?" asked Maya, with a shaking inner voice.

"I may have...seen a few...empires, in memories...?"

"Do you remember the...NMPGM?"

"Is that an acronym? ...I'm sorry, I don't know."

"That's...That's okay," lied Maya. "I'll... It's okay."

Her thoughts turned over themselves. Ah, we're both psychics. Ah, we can speak.
Ah, ha ha ha: she doesn't remember...after I got her killed—

----
"Wait! D-Don't...Don't panic! People in Arcaea, they—they d-d-don't keep their memories!"
The voice suddenly...insisted this. "...Usually. We've...met one person who...remembers some things."

"'Met'..."

"Are you in Arcaea...? Can we find you!?"

"I'm—" she shook her head, and her thoughts stopped as her heart rate began sharply rising up;
as the crack on her face lengthened. Something had flashed back into her head—
the memory of trusting a voice without a face.

Trust.
She felt that she couldn't—shouldn't trust.

----
...And still, she wanted to.

"I'm Maya..." said Maya through the air, feeling like a knife had caught her tongue. "Do you...have a name?"

Hesitantly, the other replied, "I...I-I-I've been told not to give out my name...easily..."

----
And a bitter smile found its way on Maya's face as she sent back her answer:
"Ha...haha... Yes... You shouldn't trust easily."

"...I want to, though."

"...Hm?"

"I want to trust you... communication is how we understand one another."

"..."

"Are you...still there?"

"Yeah," Maya replied. "And I think that's good...how you think."

----
"Um, uh...um..." the other voice stammered. Maya breathed in, and exhaled. She straightened her back.

"You're alright, then?" asked Maya. "In Arcaea..."

"Yes...! Are...Are you alright...?"

"Oh," Maya replied, her smile still there on her face. "No. Not at all."

----
...But the other girl didn't leave it at that. Gently, surely gently, she pestered.
She learned about this girl who had fallen out of Arcaea. Maya, too, learned about the unclear peer of hers
who she must have caused the death of.

She pestered Maya on and on in spite—or even perhaps because of the sentiments of "guilt"
that continually came back...

...until Maya's returning thoughts had become just a little warmer, and a little warmer still, each time.

----
In the quiet halls of God...

In time, Maya opened her book again, and again lifted her pen.
